Privacy policy

This policy covers TrueNAS Google Backup, a private tool with one user: its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the Google Drive API to upload the owner's files, list them and check that they match the originals.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, share or send any data to anyone.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or ads.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's.

Where data is kept

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computers and home server. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own machines.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the developer contact shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
